# Context research (2026-08-19) — facts for the visualization layer, each with its source

Every claim below feeds a visual element or a note on the page. Nothing here is
presented as a finding of the Court unless the ICC itself is the source.

## The warrants in context
- Putin is the FIRST head of state of a UN Security Council permanent member (P5) under an ICC warrant; only the second sitting head of state after Omar Al-Bashir (2009).
  - https://www.usip.org/publications/2023/03/how-iccs-warrant-putin-could-impact-ukraine-war
  - https://www.justsecurity.org/85529/the-icc-goes-straight-to-the-top-arrest-warrant-issued-for-putin/
- No suspect is in custody; the ICC does not try in absentia.
  - https://pam.int/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/PAM-Background-Information-Note-The-Non-Arrest-of-Individuals-Indicted-by-the-ICC-3.pdf

## Referrals arithmetic (43 States Parties)
- 38 states in the 2 Mar 2022 joint referral + Lithuania (1 Mar) + Japan + North Macedonia (11 Mar) + Montenegro (21 Mar) + Chile (1 Apr) = 43. Counted from the summary's own list; referral PDFs above.

## Jurisdiction milestones
- Ukraine became the 125th State Party; Statute in force for Ukraine 1 Jan 2025.
  - https://www.icc-cpi.int/news/icc-welcomes-ukraine-new-state-party
  - https://www.coalitionfortheicc.org/ukraine-becomes-125th-ICC-state-party
- Ukraine's art. 124 declaration: for 7 years it does not accept ICC jurisdiction over art. 8 war crimes likely committed by ITS OWN nationals — first state to use art. 124 this way.
  - https://brill.com/view/journals/icla/25/6/article-p1040_003.xml
  - https://voelkerrechtsblog.org/eurocentrism-in-ukraines-ratification-of-the-rome-statute-of-the-international-criminal-court/

## Cooperation and non-arrest
- Putin visited Mongolia (State Party) 3 Sep 2024; on 24 Oct 2024 Pre-Trial Chamber II found Mongolia failed to cooperate and referred it to the Assembly of States Parties; held head-of-state immunity is not opposable before the ICC.
  - https://www.icc-cpi.int/news/ukraine-situation-icc-pre-trial-chamber-ii-finds-mongolia-failed-cooperate-arrest-and

## Scale of the underlying crimes (context, NOT Court findings)
- Ukraine's official "Children of War" database: ≥19,546 children deported/displaced; 1,859 returned (Dec 2025, First Lady at Paris summit). Estimates by ombudspersons run far higher (up to 150k–300k).
  - https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-says-1-859-abducted-children-have-been-returned-zelenska-tells-paris-summit/
  - https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/vienna-international-organisations/deportation-ukrainian-children-amidst-russia%E2%80%99s-war-aggression-how-ensure-accountability-and-children_en
- Grid campaign charged in warrants 2–3: strikes from at least 10 Oct 2022 to 9 Mar 2023; winter 2022–23 attacks left ~12 million with limited or no access to energy (HRW).
  - https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/06/26/new-icc-warrants-issued-ukraine-crimes
  - https://www.justsecurity.org/97300/icc-warrants-ukraines-power-grid/
  - https://kyivindependent.com/breaking-icc-issues-arrest-warrants-for-shoigu-gerasimov/

## The aggression gap
- The ICC cannot try the crime of aggression in this situation; on 25 Jun 2025 Ukraine and the Council of Europe signed the agreement establishing the Special Tribunal for the Crime of Aggression.
