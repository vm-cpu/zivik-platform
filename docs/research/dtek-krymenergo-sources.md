## Figures (all in the verbatim text)
- Claimed: ≥ USD 421,198,000 + tax gross-up + interest at Russia's sovereign rate
- Awarded: USD 207,800,000 (by majority) + 3M USD LIBOR/SOFR + 1%, compounded annually, from 22 Jan 2015
- Costs: USD 1,362,422.88 administrative + USD 9,401,644.76 legal
- Company scale: 27,000 km², 780,000+ consumers, 23 district + 2 municipal networks, 57.6% stake (2006–2012)
